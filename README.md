# Required Materials

Tracks what items and other requirements you need to build things in **Sailing** and **Construction**.

## Adding something to your list

Click anything you want to build:

| Where | What to click |
|---|---|
| Sailing skill guide | any component or facility |
| Construction skill guide | any piece of furniture |
| Boat Customisation | **Build** or **Check Materials** |
| Furniture Creation menu | **Build** |

You can ignore tracking of things you're actively building through the plugin's settings.

## Reading the requirements

The plugin checks your bank and inventory against what each build needs. Opening the bank is required to sync your required items

<img src="docs/side-panel.png" alt="The side panel showing tracked builds" width="300">

Upgrades of furniture need the previous tier built first. Visit your POH once in **build mode** so the plugin can track the current state of build furniture. 

## Using it at the bank

Clicking the hammer button near the scrollbar will filter the items you need per piece of furniture or facility. Click it
again to put your bank back in it's original state.

<img src="docs/bank-view.png" alt="The bank grouped by build" width="600">

## Settings

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Don't track builds you can already make | on | Clicking **Build** on something you have everything for won't add it to your list |
| Clear builds when you make them | off | Building something takes it off your list |
